# Working HLA gravity gloves with wrist pockets
A faithful recreation of the gravity glove grab mechanic from Half-Life: Alyx. Also including functional wrist pockets. 

## How to use the grab
- Point at an object with an open hand. The one you're pointing at glows.
- Hold grip to lock onto it.
- Flick your wrist, and it flies toward your hand in an arc. Catch it yourself when it reaches your hand.

## Features 
- The objects will automatically rotate in the air to fit in your hand when it reaches it.
- Includes visuals and sounds closely resembling those in Half-Life: Alyx
- Extremely customizable settings for both the functions and visuals, for those who want that.

## How to use the wrist pocket
- Bring something you're holding to the pocket on your wrist and let go, then it goes in. A small copy shows what's inside.
- To bring something out, reach to a pocket with an open hand and grab to take it out, it's held the way you put it in.
- Bring something else to a full pocket to swap it, the old one drops out beside it.


## Installation
If you're not using a mod loader you can install it by hand by putting `Mods/GravityGloves.dll` in BONELAB's `Mods` folder.
